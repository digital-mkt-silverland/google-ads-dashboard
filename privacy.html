Privacy policy: Silverland Dashboard Recon

Silverland Dashboard Recon is an internal Google Apps Script used only by the Silverland Hospitality digital marketing team. It is not offered to the public.

Data the script reads

Google Ads reporting data (cost, clicks, conversions) for Silverland Hospitality's own Google Ads accounts, Google Analytics 4 reporting data for silverlandhotels.com, and a Silverland Google Sheet that lists hotel bookings.

How the data is used

The script aggregates the data by day, hotel and traffic source and writes the totals into a Silverland Google Sheet that feeds an internal performance dashboard. It does not write guest names, email addresses, phone numbers or booking numbers to that sheet. It does not change any Google Ads, Google Analytics or Google Sheets settings outside that sheet.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access to the script can be removed at any time from the Google Account security page.
